How it plays

  • Flap to stay up. The bird drifts forward on its own and the world scrolls past — grass, roads, and rivers.
  • Roads: cars drive both ways along the ground. Skim just over a roof for a near-miss +1 — touch one and the run ends.
  • Rivers: open water is a splash landing. Logs float by — you can rest on one and ride it, then flap off before it drifts away.
  • Pig towers: crates stacked on the grass with a pig on top. Drop onto the pig from above to pop it for +5 and crumble the tower. Hit the side and it's over.
  • Scoring: +1 for every stretch of road, river, or grass you cross, +3 per golden egg. The traffic speeds up the further you get.
Keys: Space / ↑ flap  ·  P pause  ·  M sound  ·  🎵 button (top right) turns the music on/off — it speeds up as you do
